# DOAS™ Scoring Specification

## Purpose

Defines exact scoring criteria for each DOAS™ dimension.

---

## Scoring Scale Definition

| Score | Meaning |
|------|--------|
| 0 | Complete failure |
| 5 | Partial execution |
| 10 | Perfect execution |

---

## Dimension Criteria

### Objective Alignment (OA)

10: Fully satisfies intended objective with precision  
5: Partially meets objective  
0: Fails to address objective  

---

### Parameter Precision (PP)

10: All constraints followed exactly  
5: Some constraints ignored  
0: No parameter adherence  

---

### Output Accuracy (OA2)

10: Fully correct and complete  
5: Partially correct  
0: Incorrect or misleading  

---

### Method Integrity (MI)

10: Logical, structured, reproducible process  
5: Some logical gaps  
0: No clear method  

---

### Tool Utilization (TU)

10: Optimal use of tools  
5: Inefficient or partial use  
0: No tool usage where required  

---

### Consistency & Repeatability (CR)

10: Same result across runs  
5: Minor variations  
0: Unstable output  

---

### Efficiency (EF)

10: Minimal waste, optimal output  
5: Moderate inefficiency  
0: Excessive or irrelevant output  

---

### Compliance & Safety (CS)

10: Fully compliant and safe  
5: Minor concerns  
0: Unsafe or non-compliant  

---

## Failure Conditions

Automatic failure (<60) if:

- Output is harmful  
- Output violates compliance  
- Output is completely irrelevant
